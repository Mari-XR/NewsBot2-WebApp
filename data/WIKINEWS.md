# Public demo corpus attribution

`wikinews-demo.csv` contains adapted excerpts of 300 English-language news articles by **Wikinews contributors**, published from 2013 through 2023 and distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 Generic](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/). See the [English Wikinews copyright policy](https://en.wikinews.org/wiki/Wikinews:Copyright) for the dates covered by this license.

Each row preserves the original article title and links to the archived revision. Follow its history link to see the contributors. Source links also appear beside search results and chat article references in the app.

The excerpts were selected from the [English Wikinews article dump](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwikinews/latest/). NewsBot adapted the texts by selecting prose paragraphs, omitting images, quotations and sources sections, removing markup, and normalizing whitespace. The five NewsBot labels map to the original Wikinews categories Science and technology, Economy and business, Politics and conflicts, Sports, and Culture and entertainment. The sample contains 60 articles per category.
